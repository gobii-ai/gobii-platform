Research methodology

How this comparison was researched.

We reviewed public materials for SeekOut Recruit, MCP, Sam, and Spot, plus Gobii’s documentation, pricing, source code, and recruiting template. Sources were checked on August 2, 2026. We prioritized first-party documentation, labeled vendor-reported statistics, and separated documented capabilities from performance claims.

We did not use an authenticated SeekOut workspace, customer interviews, or a controlled head-to-head benchmark. Recommendations describe product fit based on public evidence; they do not promise hiring outcomes. See our editorial policy for our review standards and corrections process.

Executive summary

SeekOut is a recruiting platform with AI agents. Gobii is an AI employee platform that can recruit.

The shortest useful answer: choose SeekOut when the candidate graph is the advantage. Choose Gobii when your process is the advantage. SeekOut packages talent data, recruiter search, enrichment, campaigns, rediscovery, and screening. Gobii assigns persistent AI employees to custom work across recruiting systems and the rest of the business.

SeekOut is stronger when recruiters need a large, structured talent pool on day one. It reports more than one billion public profiles, including 40 million-plus GitHub profiles, 97 million-plus academic and expert profiles, and 3.7 million-plus security-cleared profiles (SeekOut dataset overview).

Gobii is stronger when the assignment is not standardized. It can research approved portfolios, publications, directories, licensing systems, and customer databases. It can then apply a custom rubric, maintain the ATS or tracker, prepare submissions, and return on schedule. Separate employees can own sourcing, research, review, operations, and reporting.

The products can also be complementary: SeekOut supplies talent intelligence, while Gobii handles the custom operating work around it. We do not claim a verified native SeekOut–Gobii integration. Any handoff should use customer-approved data, systems, and licensing terms.

The 30-second decision

Choose the product that solves the expensive bottleneck.

Choose SeekOut if...

You need built-in candidate data, structured technical or specialty-talent pools, recruiter-native search, contact enrichment, ATS rediscovery, market insights, native campaigns, inbound applicant evaluation, or packaged AI screening. SeekOut is the more complete recruiting product out of the box.

Choose Gobii if...

You already have useful data sources and need AI employees to operate the process around them: niche research, custom qualification, browser-based systems, files, spreadsheets, reports, recurring schedules, approvals, and work that crosses recruiting, operations, sales, or research.

Use both if...

SeekOut produces a strong candidate pool, but recruiters still spend substantial time validating external evidence, applying client-specific rules, moving data among systems, packaging submissions, and reporting. Use SeekOut for talent intelligence and Gobii for the custom operating work around it.

A crucial product distinction

“SeekOut” is a product family, not one feature set.

This page primarily compares SeekOut Recruit with Gobii. Recruit covers search, talent insights, enrichment, campaigns, exports, and—on higher packages—rediscovery and screening. SeekOut MCP brings recruiting actions into compatible AI assistants. Sam evaluates inbound applicants and runs structured AI interviews. Spot combines SeekOut’s AI with human recruiters to deliver interview-ready candidates (SeekOut plan comparison).

Spot is not a like-for-like software purchase. It competes with agencies, contract recruiters, and internal recruiting capacity. Gobii supplies software and AI employees; it does not publicly offer a managed service in which human recruiters run the search and deliver a slate.

Where SeekOut wins

SeekOut gives recruiters structured talent assets on day one.

SeekOut’s clearest advantage is included talent data. Recruit Core combines unlimited search across a one-billion-plus profile index with AI search, 30-plus filters, 500 monthly contact credits, 1,000 monthly exports, email integration, and multistep campaigns. Recruiters can move from search to outreach without assembling a separate data stack (SeekOut pricing and features).

Its specialty datasets are also difficult to reproduce candidate by candidate. SeekOut publishes 40 million-plus GitHub profiles, 97 million-plus academic and expert profiles, and 3.7 million-plus cleared-talent profiles. Healthcare search adds specialty, credential, affiliation, and NPI-related fields (SeekOut dataset overview).

Higher packages add ATS rediscovery and inbound screening. Sourcing + Integration unifies external and ATS search. Full Recruiting Funnel adds job-specific evaluation and Sam screening interviews with summaries, competency scores, transcripts, and ATS records (SeekOut Sam screening guide). SeekOut says recruiters remain responsible for advancement and employment decisions (SeekOut responsible-AI policy).

SeekOut Spot supplies recruiting capacity, not just software. It fits buyers who want human recruiters and AI to run sourcing, engagement, screening, and slate delivery. Gobii does not claim an equivalent managed service (SeekOut Spot).

SeekOut’s strongest reasons to buy

  • Immediate access to a large candidate index
  • Technical and specialty-talent datasets
  • Recruiter-native search, filters, and insights
  • Included enrichment and recruiting campaigns
  • ATS rediscovery and packaged AI screening
  • Optional managed recruiting through Spot

Where Gobii wins

Your recruiting process is more than a candidate database.

SeekOut standardizes recruiting work. Many teams, however, compete on the process around the search: their niche sources, evidence rules, exception handling, systems, candidate submissions, and client reporting.

Gobii assigns that process to persistent AI employees. Each employee can have its own charter, tools, files, schedule, and approval boundaries. One can source candidates, another can inspect technical evidence, and a third can audit the shortlist. Operations and reporting employees can maintain systems and prepare recurring briefs.

Browser execution is another dividing line. SeekOut’s extension supports recruiting-oriented enrichment, saving, contact retrieval, and export (SeekOut extension guide). Gobii can operate permitted browser-only systems more broadly. That matters for association sites, licensing portals, specialist databases, legacy applications, and internal tools.

Gobii can also create custom scorecards, evidence-linked dossiers, submission packets, spreadsheets, hiring-manager memos, and market reports. These formats become part of the employee’s job instead of exports a recruiter must rework. Gobii for Teams adds shared agents, credentials, integrations, roles, and pooled capacity.

The same platform can support sales, operations, research, and customer work when hiring demand slows. Gobii’s MIT-licensed core can also be self-hosted when source inspection, model choice, or network control matters (Gobii source repository).

A stronger combined workflow

Use SeekOut as the talent intelligence layer. Use Gobii as the custom operating layer.

1. Build the candidate pool

Use SeekOut’s native search, specialized talent datasets, recruiter filters, talent insights, ATS rediscovery where licensed, and contact enrichment to create an initial pool. A recruiter reviews the search strategy and selects which records can enter the next stage.

2. Deepen permitted research

Through an approved handoff, Gobii investigates sources outside the standard record: portfolios, public writing, conference appearances, company bios, directories, licensing portals, or customer-supplied databases. It records evidence and flags unknowns instead of inventing certainty.

3. Apply your process

Gobii evaluates each candidate using hard requirements, preferences, exclusions, evidence standards, and client-specific rules. It can route ambiguous cases to a recruiter, generate follow-up questions, and create the exact scorecard or submission the team requires.

4. Complete recurring operations

SeekOut can run native recruiting campaigns and Sam screening when purchased. Gobii can maintain an approved ATS or tracker, organize files, prepare hiring-manager briefings, notify the team, generate recurring funnel reports, and begin the next sourcing cycle on schedule.

SeekOut publishes an MCP server, and Gobii can use MCP tools. Deeper interoperability is plausible, but it is not a verified public integration. Any direct connection would need validation for authentication, licensing, supported actions, data handling, and reliability. For now, the products should be described as working alongside one another through approved workflows.

Pricing reality

A lower platform price does not include SeekOut’s talent graph.

SeekOut Recruit Core starts at $149 per month when paid annually, or $179 month to month. It includes unlimited search, one billion-plus profiles, 500 monthly contact credits, 1,000 monthly exports, email integration, campaigns, and talent-pool insights. SeekOut lists three included seats and a 14-day trial without a credit card. Higher Recruit packages and Spot use custom pricing (SeekOut pricing).

Gobii Pro is $50 per month for 1,000 tasks and unlimited always-on agents. Scale is $250 per month for 10,000 tasks. Team is $50 per seat with pooled tasks, shared agents, integrations, credentials, roles, and billing. Enterprise adds dedicated infrastructure, SLAs, and custom governance. The MIT-licensed core can be self-hosted.

The prices buy different assets. SeekOut bundles recruiting data and workflow. Gobii supplies AI execution and workflow infrastructure, so customers may still need an ATS, contact provider, or licensed candidate source. Compare the total cost of producing an engaged candidate and completing the surrounding work—not the subscription alone.

Human judgment stays central

Use AI to expand evidence and capacity—not to make unsupervised hiring decisions.

SeekOut says its AI supports rather than replaces recruiter judgment. Sam produces rankings, explanations, interview records, and competency scores, but recruiters remain responsible for advancement and employment decisions (SeekOut responsible-AI policy). SeekOut publishes separate security and compliance information.

Gobii workflows should use the same principle. Give each employee narrow access, require approval for sensitive actions, preserve evidence links, and route uncertain cases to a person. Recruiters should review outputs for accuracy, privacy, accessibility, bias, and job relevance. Neither product removes the employer’s duty to run a lawful, fair hiring process.

Frequently asked questions

Common SeekOut vs Gobii buying questions.

Is Gobii an alternative to SeekOut?

Gobii is an alternative when the main need is custom research and recurring recruiting operations, not a replacement for SeekOut’s built-in candidate index. SeekOut is stronger for recruiter-native search, specialty talent data, contact enrichment, ATS rediscovery, and packaged screening.

Does Gobii include a candidate database?

No equivalent proprietary candidate graph is included with Gobii. Gobii works with permitted public, licensed, uploaded, and connected sources. SeekOut reports more than one billion candidate profiles.

Can Gobii work with SeekOut?

The products can be used alongside one another through customer-approved handoffs and systems, but Gobii does not claim a verified native SeekOut integration. SeekOut can supply the candidate pool while Gobii handles custom research, qualification, deliverables, and recurring operations.

What is the difference between SeekOut Recruit and SeekOut Spot?

Recruit is software for sourcing, engaging, rediscovering, and screening talent. Spot combines SeekOut’s AI with human recruiters. Compare Recruit when you need software and Spot when you want delivered recruiting capacity.

Does SeekOut conduct AI screening interviews?

Yes. SeekOut Sam supports structured AI interviews with summaries, competency scores, transcripts, and ATS synchronization. SeekOut states that recruiters retain hiring authority.

Which is better for technical recruiting?

SeekOut is usually stronger when indexed technical talent data and hands-on search are the priority, including its GitHub dataset. Gobii is stronger for bespoke portfolio research, client-specific evidence rules, custom reports, and cross-system work.

Which is better for a recruiting agency?

SeekOut is stronger when the agency needs a standardized sourcing, enrichment, and outreach stack or wants managed capacity through Spot. Gobii is compelling when the agency’s advantage is a client-specific qualification process, niche research, custom candidate submissions, multi-stage quality control, and recurring reporting.

Can either platform be self-hosted?

Gobii’s core platform is MIT-licensed and can be self-hosted. SeekOut is a hosted recruiting platform and does not publicly offer a self-hosted edition.

Bottom line

Choose SeekOut when talent data is core. Choose Gobii when custom workflow is core.

Choose SeekOut for immediate candidate data, specialist talent pools, recruiter search, enrichment, rediscovery, screening, or a managed recruiting service. Gobii does not reproduce those purpose-built assets.

Choose Gobii for niche research, custom evaluation, browser-based systems, specialized employee roles, recurring operations, or self-hosting. Explore Gobii for Recruiting or inspect the Candidate Sourcing AI Employee.

If both needs matter, use SeekOut for talent intelligence and Gobii for the process around it.
