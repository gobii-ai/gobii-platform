QUESTIONS

Before you hire

Do I have to build or prompt anything?

No. You pick an agent that already knows the job, set your criteria in plain English, and results start arriving. There's nothing to assemble — and every agent takes plain-English direction after you hire it, so it can be shaped into exactly the custom agent you need.

Where do the results go?

Wherever you work: your ATS (Greenhouse and others), CRM, email, Slack, or a spreadsheet — on the schedule you choose.

What does it cost?

Start with a 7-day free trial. After that, plans scale with how much work your agents do — see pricing above.

Does it work with LinkedIn Recruiter?

Yes — teams with LinkedIn Recruiter seats can connect them, and your recruiting agent works within your Recruiter workflows. It also sources from LinkedIn data, the open web, and GitHub, so coverage doesn't depend on any single source.

What if the results miss the mark?

Tell the agent, like you would a new hire. It adjusts its criteria and the next delivery reflects it.
