Definition

What is an AI sales agent?

An AI sales agent performs multi-step sales work such as prospect research, qualification, enrichment, and outreach preparation. Gobii focuses on supervised execution: it gathers evidence and prepares review-ready outputs while sales teams retain control over targeting, messaging, and sending.

What it does

Give the AI teammate a defined sales workflow

The best AI sales agent work is concrete. Give Gobii the inputs, approved sources, output format, schedule, and review points. The agent handles the repetitive browser and data work around your pipeline.

Prospecting

Turn ICP criteria into recurring searches across approved sources. Collect accounts and contacts that look like a match.

Qualification

Compare each lead against target industries, roles, company size, geography, tech stack, funding signals, and disqualifiers.

Account research

Prepare account briefs with source links, company context, recent signals, decision-maker notes, and why each prospect may matter.

Outreach prep

Draft talking points and first-pass messages. Sellers can edit, approve, reject, or move them into a sales engagement tool.

Evaluation checklist

How to evaluate an AI sales agent

Evaluate the first reviewed output and the operating controls, not only a feature list or polished demo.

Questions to use when testing an AI SDR, AI lead-generation agent, or account-research automation workflow.
Check What to inspect
Source freshnessCan you see when the underlying page or record was published, updated, or retrieved?
Evidence traceabilityDoes each material claim or fit note retain a source URL or record reference?
ICP controls and disqualifiersCan the workflow enforce titles, segments, exclusions, negative examples, and must-have criteria?
Permission scopeCan access stay limited to the sources, files, contacts, tools, and actions the workflow needs?
Human approval pointsCan a seller review targeting, evidence, contact data, messaging, and the handoff before outreach?
Contact-data verificationDoes the workflow distinguish discovered data from data that has been independently verified?
CRM and CSV handoffCan approved output move downstream with stable fields, source links, reviewer status, and notes intact?
Failure and escalation behaviorDoes the agent flag blocked sources, missing evidence, ambiguity, and tasks that require a person?
Usage or pricing modelIs cost tied to seats, records, tasks, messages, or execution, and can the team cap early usage?
Time to first reviewed outputHow much setup is required before a seller can inspect a real batch for a narrow brief?
Reviewer feedbackCan accepted and rejected examples refine sources, criteria, disqualifiers, and output format?

Workflow ownership

Use an AI sales employee where repetition slows the team down

Gobii is not a black-box replacement for sales judgment. It is a persistent AI teammate for research and preparation, so sellers can spend more time where context and trust matter.

Inputs

ICP criteria, priority accounts, connected sheets, source lists, CRM exports, market notes, and past lead feedback.

Actions

Search sources, inspect pages, enrich records, compare fit, summarize findings, and prepare review-ready outputs.

Outputs

Lead lists, account briefs, source URLs, fit notes, enrichment fields, outreach drafts, and handoff summaries.

Review

Sales or RevOps checks fit, verifies sources, edits messaging, and decides which prospects should enter active outreach.

Where it fits

Automate the middle of sales research, not the human relationship

Sales teams already have databases, CRMs, sequencing tools, and call tools. The slow work sits between them: finding context, checking fit, cleaning records, and keeping handoffs current. That is where an AI sales agent helps.

Sales research is one example of a broader operating model. See how Gobii deploys AI employees across supervised business workflows.

Sheets and CSV

Write structured batches to Google Sheets or CSV. Include reviewer status, disqualifiers, enrichment fields, and source links.

Webhooks

First reviewed batch

What happens on your first run?

Start with one narrow segment and a deliverable your seller can inspect. Use that review to refine the next run.

Step 1

Define the brief

Specify industries, titles, geography, company size, signals, exclusions, and examples of good leads.

Step 2

Choose sources

Select approved websites, company lists, documents, sheets, CRM exports, or webhook-provided inputs.

Step 3

Review the batch

Inspect source links, fit notes, enrichment fields, disqualifiers, and suggested outreach angles.

Step 4

Decide the handoff

Approve, reject, revise, export, verify contact data, or narrow the next run.
