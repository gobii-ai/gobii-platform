Workflow

From role brief to evidence-backed shortlist

Gobii turns top-of-funnel recruiting research into a repeatable workflow. The recruiter defines the target and evidence standard; the agent searches and organizes the work; the recruiter reviews what should happen next.

What the recruiter controls: accept or reject a recommendation, inspect its provenance, correct the search criteria, request another pass, and approve any outreach.

1

Define the target profile

Provide the role, required skills, exclusions, seniority, location, and the evidence standard for a useful match.

2

Search approved sources

The agent researches the public, professional, connected, or team-provided sources the customer has authorized.

3

Qualify with evidence

Each profile is compared with job-related criteria. Supporting evidence, conflicts, and uncertainty remain visible.

4

Export the shortlist

Move recruiter-ready fields into a supported handoff such as Google Sheets or CSV, then review before the next step.

Trust and human control

Automate the research, not the employment decision

Gobii prepares evidence-linked recruiting work for people to review. The hiring team sets the criteria, owns corrections, and decides whether a candidate should advance.

{% lucide "user-check" class="h-5 w-5" %}

Recruiter approval stays in the workflow

Recruiters review candidate fit and approve consequential outreach. Interviews and hiring decisions remain with the organization.

{% lucide "scan-search" class="h-5 w-5" %}

Recommendations show their work

Each match can include source provenance, job-related evidence, conflicts, missing information, and questions to verify.

{% lucide "sliders-horizontal" class="h-5 w-5" %}

Requirements and exclusions are configurable

Teams can correct a match by refining role criteria, exclusions, location rules, evidence standards, or review notes and then rerunning the research.

{% lucide "shield-check" class="h-5 w-5" %}

Sources must be permitted

Customers choose approved sources and connected tools. Recruiters should confirm that collection, use, and outreach follow their policies and applicable requirements.

What Gobii does not automate: it does not independently make employment decisions, replace recruiter judgment, or turn uncertain research into a guaranteed assessment of a person.

Use cases

Candidate sourcing workflows built around a specific hiring need

The strongest automated candidate sourcing workflows define the input, the research job, and the deliverable before the agent starts.

Niche technical roles

Input
Technical requirements and evidence standards.
Work
Research public role and project evidence.
Output
A source-labelled shortlist with gaps to verify.

Location-constrained searches

Input
Location, time-zone, travel, or license constraints.
Work
Check available location evidence against the brief.
Output
A review queue that flags uncertain or conflicting locations.

Executive and hard-to-fill roles

Input
Leadership scope, sector context, and exclusions.
Work
Assemble relevant role history and public evidence.
Output
A deeper research brief for recruiter validation.

Agency sourcing lists

Input
A client brief, rubric, and delivery format.
Work
Apply the same documented criteria across a search.
Output
A consistent, export-ready client shortlist.

Pipeline refreshes

Input
An approved cadence and saved role criteria.
Work
Repeat the search and compare newly found evidence.
Output
A refresh that identifies additions, changes, and open questions.
